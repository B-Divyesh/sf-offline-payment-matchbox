How Matchbox Ledger stores your data

Matchbox Ledger reconciles financial files without sending them to us. This policy explains local storage and license checks.

Your reconciliation data

Invoice rows, payment rows, matches, and notes stay in browser IndexedDB. They are not sent to Matchbox Ledger, Sociobot, a bank, or an analytics service. “Keep a copy” stores the selected CSV text locally. This option is off by default.

Backups and deletion

Your browser creates exported backups and reports locally. You choose where each file is saved. “Clear local workspace” removes this browser’s workspace data. Clearing browser site data does the same.

License checks

Buying or restoring Plus saves its license token in local storage. The app sends only that token to Sociobot for daily verification. Reconciliation files and totals are never included.

Network and tracking

The installed app caches its own files for offline use. We include no advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
