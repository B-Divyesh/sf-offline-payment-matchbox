Privacy, kept simple.

Matchbox Ledger is designed to reconcile financial files without sending them to us. This policy explains what stays on your device and the one case where the app contacts our billing service.

Your reconciliation data

Invoice rows, payment rows, matches, and notes are parsed and stored in IndexedDB in your browser. They are not transmitted to Matchbox Ledger, Sociobot, a bank, or an analytics service. The optional “keep a copy” checkbox stores the selected CSV text locally; it is off by default.

Backups and deletion

When you export a backup or report, your browser creates the file locally. You choose where it is saved. “Clear local workspace” removes the workspace data from this browser. Clearing site data in your browser does the same.

License checks

If you buy or restore Matchbox Plus, the app stores the license token in local storage and sends that token to the Sociobot billing API no more than once per day to confirm it is active. Reconciliation files and totals are never included. Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout as merchant of record under its own purchase records and legal obligations.

Network and tracking

The installed app caches its own files for offline use. We include no advertising cookies, behavioral analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Questions

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@sociobot.in.
